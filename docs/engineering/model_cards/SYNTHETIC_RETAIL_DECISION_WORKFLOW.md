## Intended and prohibited use

Use the profile only to reproduce and study the bounded local synthetic
decision workflow. Do not use it to order stock, rank business policies,
forecast a real product, report real forecast skill, or estimate a future
prediction interval.

## Inputs and algorithm

The workflow requires seven distinct consecutive positive product-day counts
whose declared release dates are on or before the requested `as_of`. The
values are integers. The existing `project_seasonal_naive_lag7` repeatedly
applies the existing one-step seasonal-naive rule: each next value is copied
from seven positions earlier. The result is a 28-day tuple consisting of four
repetitions of the latest eligible week.

There is no source authentication, imputation, parameter fitting, automatic
model selection, online update or uncertainty adjustment. Missing or
unpublished history causes an abstention before simulation.

## Data and temporal evaluation

All input workbook bytes and event counts are generated by
`synthetic_fixtures.py`; the dates in its release table are invented. The
reader's digest proves only identity of those exact local bytes. It does not
prove a publisher, legal rights, row completeness or historical availability.

For an `as_of` date of 2011-01-26, M1F-02 runs the existing matched-origin
historical diagnostic at 2011-01-19. Its seven-day holdout is 2011-01-13
through 2011-01-19. The separate 28-day profile uses the seven released
feature dates 2011-01-20 through 2011-01-26. The fixed baseline comparison
includes `seasonal_naive_lag7` and `trailing_mean_lag7` on the same eligible
historical targets. Returned scores are descriptive fixture diagnostics and
do not select which method produces the profile.

No approved real-source snapshot or empirical holdout is used. Consequently,
this card does not report a real-data MAE, RMSE, WAPE, forecast skill or
generalization claim. Any metric printed by the example is computed only from
the fictional fixture.

## Uncertainty

M1F-02 reuses M1D-01's fixed 21-day calibration target window immediately
before its seven-day historical holdout, with origin-specific declared-release
checks and a minimum of five eligible residuals. The diagnostic is explicitly
`heuristic_envelope_not_prediction_interval`. Its coverage is retrospective,
not nominal; the workflow leaves `nominal_coverage` and
`calibrated_prediction_interval` unset and does not attach a radius to the
28-day profile. The fixture does not establish empirical coverage, calibrated
uncertainty, a confidence level, or a guarantee.

## Decision and simulation context

The profile feeds three existing inventory-policy rules in the local M1E-03
test double. Each policy sees the same seeded exogenous demand streams across
the three lead-time cases. The output is conditional arithmetic under declared
starting stock, lead time and synthetic cost coefficients. No policy winner
is selected. WorldKernel is not called, and the decision trace marks missing
source rights, empirical model validation, calibrated uncertainty and actual
engine evidence.

## Known limitations and upgrade gates

Before any real forecast capability is claimed, obtain approved source rights,
immutable snapshot and independently supported availability/revision evidence;
predeclare a temporal holdout; compare candidates on identical eligible
targets; report abstention, baselines, metrics and uncertainty honestly; and
complete human/model review. Before claiming an actual policy execution,
complete M1E-02 with a pinned compatible WorldKernel runtime, strict input
mapping, bounded execution and validated engine output. Those gates remain
open under B-01, B-02 and B-04.
